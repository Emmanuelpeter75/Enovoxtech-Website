Everything you need to know about E-NovoxTech — our mission, our technology, and how we are shaping the future of AI.

Company

E-NovoxTech is an emerging African AI startup focused on researching, building, and developing artificial intelligence solutions that address real-world challenges. We are currently building Enovox AI while laying the foundation for future AI infrastructure designed to serve businesses, developers, and communities across Africa.

E-NovoxTech was founded by Emmanuel Peter (Emmytoonz), a Nigerian AI developer and entrepreneur with a vision to build trusted AI technology that empowers Africa through innovation, integrity, and continuous learning.

E-NovoxTech focuses on building practical AI solutions for Africa's real-world challenges rather than creating technology for its own sake. We combine research, innovation, and local understanding to develop AI products that are accessible, relevant, and impactful. As an early-stage company, we believe in growing with integrity—building step by step, learning continuously, and creating technology that empowers people, businesses, and communities across Africa.

Mission & Vision

Africa is home to unique opportunities and challenges that are often underserved by existing technology. We believe AI should understand local contexts, cultures, languages, and business environments. Our mission is to develop solutions that empower African businesses, creators, students, and communities while contributing to the continent's technological future..

Our long-term vision is to become a leading African AI company that builds trusted AI infrastructure and intelligent products for businesses, developers, and communities. We envision an ecosystem where African innovators can access technology designed with African realities in mind, enabling them to build solutions that create lasting impact.

Products & Technology

Our current research focuses on:

  • Sales & Predictive Analytics: Helping businesses forecast revenue.
  • Churn Prediction: Identifying at-risk customers before they leave.
  • Intelligent Automation: Reducing manual workload in administrative tasks.
  • Enovox AI: Our proprietary intelligent agent system.

We are currently in the Research & Development (R&D) phase. We are conducting private testing on several models. Join our waitlist to be notified when we move into public Beta.

We believe trust is essential when building AI. As our products evolve, we are committed to implementing strong security practices, responsible data handling, and transparent privacy policies that protect user information and comply with applicable regulations.
